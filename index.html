Free AI waifu chat in your browser

        
WaifuAI is an AI waifu chat you open in any browser. Pick an anime character, say hi, and she answers in her own personality. The hosted version comes with a free AI model and free voices, so there is no subscription to pay for and no account to create.


        
An anime AI chatbot with a Live2D body

        
Most AI waifu chatbots are a profile picture next to text. In WaifuAI your companion is an animated Live2D character who blinks, moves and reacts while you talk. Choose from more than 50 Live2D models, or add your own.


        
Talk to her with your voice

        
Your AI waifu reads her replies out loud, with several voices and languages to choose from, including Kokoro voices that run on your own device. Hold the microphone button to talk back instead of typing.


        
Make your own waifu

        
Write her persona: who she is, how she talks and what she likes. She keeps memory of your conversations, so she remembers you between sessions. All of it is stored in your browser, not on a server.


        
Pictures and backgrounds

        
Ask her to draw something, or for a selfie, and she sends you a picture. The background follows the story too: move the chat to a beach at sunset and the scene changes with you.


        
Chat in your language

        
She understands and answers in many languages, including Russian, Japanese, Spanish, German and Chinese. Pick your language in the settings and she replies in it.

    


    
Questions

    

        

            Is WaifuAI free?
            
Yes. WaifuAI is free and open source under the MIT-0 license. The hosted version includes a free LLM and free text-to-speech, so there is nothing to pay for and no trial to expire.

        

        

            Do I need an account to use WaifuAI?
            
No. There is no sign-up, no email and no login. Open the app and start chatting. Your conversations and settings are stored in your own browser.

        

        

            Does WaifuAI work on a phone?
            
Yes. WaifuAI runs in the mobile browser with a layout built for small screens. There is no app to install on Android or iOS.

        

        

            Can I use my own AI model?
            
Yes. Bring your own OpenRouter API key and pick any model it offers, or point WaifuAI at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a local one. See the provider docs.

        

        

            Does my AI waifu remember our conversations?
            
Yes. WaifuAI keeps conversation memory and a customizable persona, so your companion stays consistent between sessions. Memory lives in your browser, not on a server.

        

        

            Do I need to log in to WaifuAI?
            
No. There is no login page and no password. WaifuAI opens straight into the chat, and nothing you type is tied to an account.

        

        

            How is WaifuAI different from other AI waifu apps?
            
WaifuAI is free, needs no account, runs in the browser without an install, and is open source, so you can read the code or run it yourself. Your companion is an animated Live2D character with a voice, not just a chat window.

        

        

            Can I change the character, voice and background?
            
Yes. Several Live2D models ship with the app, and you can change the voice and language, the background image, and the persona from the settings panel.
